Privacy Policy — Polash AI OS

Polash IT Solutions · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Overview

Polash AI OS is a personal productivity dashboard developed by Polash IT Solutions. This policy explains what information the app does — and does not — collect.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data. The app works fully offline. Any preferences or notes you create are stored only on your own device (in the app/browser storage) and are never sent to us.

Permissions

Microphone (optional): used only for the voice-command feature, only while you actively use it. Voice is processed by your device/browser speech service; we receive no audio recordings.

Internet: used only when you tap external links (for example Fiverr, WhatsApp, YouTube, Facebook), which open in your browser or the respective app.

Third-party links

The app contains links to third-party services (Fiverr, Upwork, WhatsApp, YouTube, Facebook, etc.). Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
